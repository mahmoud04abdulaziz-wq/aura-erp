Problem Definition The current manual methods of assigning tasks within teams rely heavily on a manager's subjective judgment and limited visibility into the team's day-to-day capacity. This approach fails to account for the dynamic nature of employee workloads and the nuanced skill sets required for specific deliverables. Also current methods for managing and delegating tasks in professional or educational environments are often inefficient and lack transparency. This leads to delays, user frustration, and a lack of data for performance improvement. Issues Issue weight Manual assignment relies heavily on a manager's subjective judgment and limited visibility into day-to-day capacity. 10 Current approaches fail to account for the dynamic nature of employee workloads and nuanced skill sets. 9 Task management processes are inefficient and lack transparency, leading to user frustration. 8 4.Inefficient task delegation leads to significant project delays 7 5.severe lack of data available for performance improvement. 6 Objectives Replace subjective manual assignment with an AI-driven, automated task routing system to provide managers with real-time visibility into day-to-day capacity. Develop a database of employee skill sets and competencies that allows the system to suggest or match specific tasks to the most qualified available team member. Build a user-facing dashboard where requesters can monitor the live status of their tasks, reducing communication gaps and increasing user confidence in the process. Reduce project delays and improve delivery speed by utilizing intelligent routing to eliminate bottlenecks in task delegation. Generate comprehensive analytics and progress reports to provide managers with actionable data for continuous performance improvement. Requirements The system must ensure data security and protect the privacy of all users. The system must be intuitive and user-friendly, allowing non-technical users to navigate and interact with it easily. The analytics dashboard must be restricted to administrators only. Constraints Vision Document Problem Description The current process of manually allocating tasks and managing employee workloads is increasingly inefficient. As operations expand and projects grow in complexity, managers struggle to constantly balance workloads, accurately match tasks to the most qualified employees, and track real-time availability. This manual approach often leads to workflow bottlenecks, unequal distribution of work, employee burnout, and underutilized skill sets. Furthermore, managers spend excessive administrative time trying to optimize scheduling rather than focusing on strategic goals. An AI-driven solution is needed to automate and optimize these delegation processes, ensuring that resources are used efficiently and tasks are assigned to the right people at the right time. System Capabilities Provide intelligent task assignment. Allocate tasks automatically based on employee workload and real-time availability. Match tasks to specific employee skill sets and competencies. Include AI-driven delegation support. Generate smart suggestions for managers to assist with complex task delegation. Learn from past project outcomes to improve future task-matching recommendations. Provide progress tracking and reporting capabilities. Allow managers to track task progress and project milestones in real-time. Generate comprehensive reports on resource utilization, efficiency, and team performance. Support employee feedback and workload management. Allow employees to provide direct feedback on assigned tasks and project scope. Enable employees to flag their capacity limits to proactively manage workload and prevent burnout. The system will adhere to the following legal pillars: Data Privacy &amp; Personal Data Protection: The system will be designed to comply with Jordanian Personal Data Protection Law No. 24 of 2023 . It will ensure that employee data (performance metrics, attendance, and task history) is processed only for professional purposes. Data encryption and explicit user consent during the first login will be implemented to protect &quot;sensitive&quot; personal data. Labor Law Compliance: The system’s delegation logic will respect the Jordanian Labor Law regarding working hours, overtime, and mandatory rest periods. The system will be configured to prevent the assignment of non-emergency tasks outside of official working hours, ensuring that the technology does not facilitate labor violations. Auditability &amp; Accountability: To provide &quot;Legal Visibility,&quot; the system will maintain an immutable audit log . This ensures that every task assigned, accepted, or rejected is recorded with a timestamp. This log can serve as legal evidence in the event of workplace disputes, safety investigations, or performance-related legal inquiries. Intellectual Property (IP) Rights: The development team commits to using only open-source libraries with compatible licenses (e.g., MIT, Apache) or properly licensed commercial software. All documentation and code produced will respect the intellectual property policies of the University of Jordan . Software Engineering Ethics: The system will follow the ACM/IEEE Code of Ethics, ensuring Confidentiality (only authorized managers see performance data) and Transparency (users are aware of how their task completion rates are being calculated). These documents will comply with both university IT regulations and national legal require-ments. Recommended Solution and Expected Project Deliverables To address the inefficiencies in manual task assignment and the lack of transparency in workload management, the proposed solution is a Smart Task Delegation System . This web-based platform will allow users to report tasks or issues directly to a centralized system, streamlining the process of submission, delegation, and execution. By utilizing this system, the organization will eliminate the subjective judgment of manual assignments and ensure that tasks are distributed based on real-time data, employee availability, and specific skill sets. Requesters will be able to provide comprehensive details, including descriptions, urgency levels, and location, ensuring the team has all necessary information from the start. The system will feature a centralized dashboard where managers can review, prioritize, and delegate tasks to the most suitable staff members. To ensure continuous communication, the system will provide real-time status updates and automated notifications, keeping all stakeholders informed from the moment a task is submitted until its successful completion. Expected Deliverables: The project team (consisting of 3 members) is committed to providing a full suite of deliverables that satisfy both the analytical and practical requirements of the course: Task Submission &amp; Reporting: The system will provide a structured interface for users to submit tasks. Each submission will act as a &quot;Task Ticket&quot; containing a title, detailed description, priority level, and location, ensuring that no critical information is missed before the delegation process begins. Centralized Review &amp; Delegation Dashboard: An administrative dashboard will serve as the core analysis tool for managers. It will allow for the evaluation of each task to prevent redundancy and ensure validity. The system will provide visibility into the current workload of each team member, enabling the manager to delegate tasks effectively to the person with the right expertise and availability. Automated Status Tracking &amp; Transparency: To resolve user frustration and improve service quality, the system will include a real-time communication engine. Requesters will receive automated notifications when their task is &quot;Received,&quot; &quot;Assigned,&quot; &quot;In-Progress,&quot; or &quot;Completed,&quot; building a transparent relationship between the management and the users. Database Design &amp; Historical Documentation: A robust database (PostgreSQL) will be implemented to store all task records. This documentation will provide a clear audit trail of who reported the issue, who resolved it, and how long it took. This historical data will serve as a vital resource for future resource planning and organizational decision-making. Performance &amp; Workload Analytics: The system will track key performance indicators (KPIs), such as average resolution time and team member utilization rates. By identifying bottlenecks in the delegation process, the organization can ensure that the maintenance or service teams remain responsive and continue to improve their overall operational reputation. Project Documentation &amp; Prototype: As part of the final delivery, a comprehensive technical report will be provided, alongside a high-fidelity UI/UX prototype. This documentation will cover the complete analysis and design phases, including DFDs, OOAD models (Class and Sequence diagrams), and ERDs to demonstrate the system's structural and behavioral logic. Local and Global Impact of the Proposed Solution Locally: The Smart Task Delegation System will ensure accuracy and fairness in workload distribution, enabling management to plan and schedule tasks more efficiently through automatically generated assignments based on real-time availability and employee expertise. Implementing these best practices will significantly improve internal service quality; automation leads to a better experience for everyone involved. It will reduce delays in handling requests, eliminate manual paperwork, and ensure that every task is managed from submission to completion through automated tracking tools. This results in optimized resource management, higher operational efficiency, and increased satisfaction for both the staff performing the tasks and the users requesting them, as they stay updated without any delay in feedback. Globally: This project contributes directly to global digital transformation and corporate sustainability efforts. It is becoming essential for organizations of all sizes to rely on intelligent systems to allocate resources efficiently and maximize the performance of their human and technical assets. A well-designed task delegation system demonstrates how technology enables organizations to make data-driven decisions to achieve superior operational efficiency. By centralizing all delegation and tracking processes into a single digital platform, it reduces time wastage and human effort lost in unorganized manual operations, setting a standard for modern, agile management. Unlike traditional project management tools that rely on manual triage, this system functions as an autonomous orchestration layer that bridges the gap between managers and employees. The system utilizes Natural Language Processing (NLP) to parse unstructured task descriptions and Machine Learning (ML) models to evaluate a multidimensional matrix of employee skill sets, historical delivery velocity, and real-time availability. Its primary purpose is to eliminate administrative bottlenecks and subjective bias by ensuring that every task is routed to the individual best equipped to complete it at that specific moment. Core System Operations: Intelligent Ingestion: Captures tasks from various inputs (text, voice, or documents) and extracts key metadata such as urgency and required expertise. Dynamic Routing: Employs an AI matching engine to rank and assign personnel based on current workloads and competency alignment. Visibility &amp; Analytics: Provides managers with live dashboards for progress tracking and predictive forecasting to identify potential project risks before they occur.
